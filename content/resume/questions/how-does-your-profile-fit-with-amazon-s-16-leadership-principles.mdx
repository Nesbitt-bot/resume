title: How does your profile fit with Amazon's 16 Leadership Principles?
order: 1
answer: |-
  Note: I am mapping this to what my projects actually demonstrate rather than stretching them. My evidence skews toward internal stakeholders and teammates instead of long-running external customers, so I say so plainly where a principle is only a partial fit.

  **Customer Obsession** — I have not run a product with long-running external users, so I map this to the specific person or team I build for and treat their needs as the spec. For [SophiasPath](/projects/sophiaspath/) I built the blog around a single content provider’s publishing workflow; on [MTA Strategy Optimizer](/projects/mta-strategy-optimizer/) I treated the algorithm team as my customer and rebuilt the data pipeline because they could not do reliable work without deterministic data.

  **Ownership** — I step into gaps that are not formally mine and see them through. On [MTA Strategy Optimizer](/projects/mta-strategy-optimizer/) I inherited a non-deterministic data setup, rebuilt the entire pipeline, and then reorganized the team around shared standards; on [Web Development & Personal Website](/projects/web-development-personal-website/) I have owned a self-hosted cloud end to end since 2020, including migrating it through several generations of infrastructure.

  **Invent and Simplify** — I look for the simpler mechanism that still works. [ImGenHCT](/projects/imgenhct/) compressed text-to-image generation into a single step instead of iterative denoising; [circtime](/projects/circtime/) monitors services from one config file with no dedicated backend; and I simplified [Web Development & Personal Website](/projects/web-development-personal-website/) until a full migration takes about 30 minutes, where the old setup could not be moved at all. As a secondary research example, [Flow-of-Thought](/projects/flow-of-thought/) extended Graph of Thoughts with relevance filtering, independent solution agents, checking, and aggregation.

  **Are Right, A Lot** — I do not claim to be right by intuition; I make the evaluation function explicit and let evidence arbitrate. In [ATTPDRL](/projects/attpdrl/) I helped analyze contradictory reward ablations, reported plainly when the 90-degree task did not converge, and revised our assumption that a trajectory-free agent should learn the whole driving problem; in [ImGenHCT](/projects/imgenhct/) I used VQA and CLIP evaluation alongside qualitative inspection; and on [MTA Strategy Optimizer](/projects/mta-strategy-optimizer/) I made the early call that reliable data had to come before modeling, which is why I rebuilt the pipeline first.

  **Learn and Be Curious** — I keep expanding what I can build. [Applets for Algorithms](/projects/applets-for-algorithms/) is maintained alongside a top-0.64% LeetCode record reached without AI; [NoteNextra (Notechondria)](/projects/notenextra-notechondria/) is my running memory archive; and on [MTA Strategy Optimizer](/projects/mta-strategy-optimizer/) I taught myself multi-touch attribution on the job. [Flow-of-Thought](/projects/flow-of-thought/) is a secondary example: I independently extended a multi-agent reasoning framework, evaluated it across three task families, and documented the mixed evidence and missing ablations.

  **Hire and Develop the Best** — I raise the people around me. Through [Washington University Chinese Groundbreakers](/projects/washington-university-chinese-groundbreakers/) I ran LeetCode and course-planning sessions for a 300+ member community and helped 15 students plan schedules; on [MTA Strategy Optimizer](/projects/mta-strategy-optimizer/) I held one-on-one meetings, arranged members by technical background, and unblocked at least five of them; and on [Gray-75](/projects/gray-75/) I supported junior developers over the long term.

  **Insist on the Highest Standards** — I hold output to a bar I am willing to sign. On [MTA Strategy Optimizer](/projects/mta-strategy-optimizer/) I refused to ship non-standardized, non-deterministic work and rebuilt it to a documented standard; [Applets for Algorithms](/projects/applets-for-algorithms/) reflects a no-AI top-0.64% LeetCode standard; [Robot Open Autonomous Racing (ROAR)](/projects/robot-open-autonomous-racing-roar/) earned the fastest single lap; and [circtime](/projects/circtime/) keeps a year of uptime history on 15-minute checks.

  **Think Big** — I choose directions that matter beyond a single deliverable. [Web Development & Personal Website](/projects/web-development-personal-website/) is a bet that infrastructure should be portable and vendor-independent; [ImGenHCT](/projects/imgenhct/) pushed one-step generation; and [MTA Strategy Optimizer](/projects/mta-strategy-optimizer/) aimed to standardize how a whole team works rather than only my own part.

  **Bias for Action** — I ship, then learn from the response. On [MTA Strategy Optimizer](/projects/mta-strategy-optimizer/) I delivered the first reproducible simulation baseline in two days rather than wait for a formal cross-team mandate; and I reworked [Web Development & Personal Website](/projects/web-development-personal-website/) until a full migration took about 30 minutes.

  **Frugality** — I accomplish more with less. [Web Development & Personal Website](/projects/web-development-personal-website/) replaces paid services with self-hosted open source, and its portable container design reduced a full service migration to about 30 minutes. [circtime](/projects/circtime/) is an experimental extension that tests whether GitHub Actions, a state branch, and static Pages hosting can support topology-aware monitoring without a dedicated backend.

  **Earn Trust** — I build trust by delivering and being candid. On [MTA Strategy Optimizer](/projects/mta-strategy-optimizer/) the team now works on the standard I established; [SophiasPath](/projects/sophiaspath/) shipped a working site for a content provider; and through [Washington University Chinese Groundbreakers](/projects/washington-university-chinese-groundbreakers/) I earned trust by reliably running events for a 300+ member community.

  **Dive Deep** — I stay connected to the details. I rebuilt [Web Development & Personal Website](/projects/web-development-personal-website/) down to FreeBSD jail permissions before moving it to Docker; in [circtime](/projects/circtime/) I directed and debugged the HTTP/TCP probes, DNS discovery, and dependency-diagnosis experiment; and in [ATTPDRL](/projects/attpdrl/) I tested PPO and DDPG, built the continuous-action adapter, reviewed the environment, maintained the documentation and visualizations, and jointly analyzed the final DQN experiments.

  **Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit** — I do not have a dramatic disagreement anecdote, and I am honest about that. My backbone shows up as holding a quality line: on [MTA Strategy Optimizer](/projects/mta-strategy-optimizer/) I refused to put my name on non-standardized work and rebuilt it rather than accept the team’s default. Once a direction is set I commit fully, as I did through repeated migrations of [Web Development & Personal Website](/projects/web-development-personal-website/).

  **Deliver Results** — I convert effort into outcomes. The mentor selected my [MTA Strategy Optimizer](/projects/mta-strategy-optimizer/) implementation as the project's most mature product and best match for the expected deliverable; [Robot Open Autonomous Racing (ROAR)](/projects/robot-open-autonomous-racing-roar/) placed second with the fastest lap; and [ImGenHCT](/projects/imgenhct/) reached a 0.79 VQA score.

  **Strive to be Earth’s Best Employer** — I try to make teams safer and more capable. On [MTA Strategy Optimizer](/projects/mta-strategy-optimizer/) I bridged remote and on-site members who were not communicating and arranged people by their technical backgrounds; [Washington University Chinese Groundbreakers](/projects/washington-university-chinese-groundbreakers/) became a 300+ member inclusive community; and on [Gray-75](/projects/gray-75/) I supported junior developers over the long term.

  **Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility** — I build things meant to outlive their immediate use. [Applets for Algorithms](/projects/applets-for-algorithms/) and [Graphical Applets for Computational Geometry Algorithms](/projects/graphical-applets-for-computational-geometry-algorithms/) are open teaching tools; [Gray-75](/projects/gray-75/) raises awareness of Alzheimer’s disease; and [Using Machine Learning to Analyze Apartheid in South Africa](/projects/using-machine-learning-to-analyze-apartheid-in-south-africa/) applied ML to historical analysis.
