## Overview

An ongoing Capgemini ad-strategy-optimization project where I rebuilt the data simulation pipeline, standardized team workflow, and unblocked five team members.

## Highlights

- Rebuilt a non-deterministic data pipeline into a fully configurable, deterministic simulation dataset so models could be evaluated on reliable synthetic data.
- Moved to the algorithm team to work on multi-touch attribution (MTA) and strategy optimization.
- Reorganized team members through one-on-one meetings based on their technical backgrounds, establishing shared adapters and standards across the data, strategy evaluation, and knowledge base groups.

## Project narrative

### Situation

The ad strategy optimization team lacked a shared, deterministic data source, and remote and on-site members with mostly non-CS backgrounds were working to different standards.

### Task

Make the data pipeline reliable and standardized, then help the team converge on shared outputs.

### Action

I rebuilt the data simulation into a fully configurable, deterministic pipeline in two days, moved to the algorithm team for multi-touch attribution and strategy optimization, and reorganized members around their technical backgrounds with shared adapters and standards across groups.

### Result

At least five team members were unblocked from their dependencies and the team converged on one standard with documented logs and outcomes.
